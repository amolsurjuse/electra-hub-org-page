Privacy at ElectraHub

Last updated: June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ElectraHub collects, uses, protects, and shares information when people use our websites, driver portal, admin portal, mobile apps, charging services, support tools, and related APIs.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account information, contact details, authentication data, charging session activity, charger and connector telemetry, payment and wallet state, support messages, notification preferences, device identifiers, and usage logs needed to operate the platform.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and protect accounts.
  • Start, monitor, stop, bill, and support charging sessions.
  • Show driver and admin dashboards, receipts, alerts, and reports.
  • Send transactional notifications such as account verification, password reset, charging status, receipts, and support updates.
  • Improve reliability, detect abuse, troubleshoot incidents, and comply with legal obligations.

3. OAuth and Social Login

If you sign in with Google or Facebook, ElectraHub receives identity information such as your name, email address, profile identifier, and verification status according to the permissions you approve. We use this data only for authentication, account creation, and account recovery.

4. Charging and Telemetry Data

Charging operations may include connector status, OCPP/OCPI identifiers, meter values, energy delivered, estimated cost, heartbeat age, session state, location metadata, and command results. This data is used to provide live charging updates, receipts, diagnostics, and operational dashboards.

5. Payments

ElectraHub may store wallet balances, top-up history, payment preferences, and masked card details. Full payment card data should be handled by the payment provider and not stored directly by ElectraHub unless a compliant payment storage arrangement is explicitly introduced.

6. Sharing

We share information with service providers that help us operate authentication, hosting, notifications, analytics, payment processing, customer support, and security monitoring. We do not sell personal information.

7. Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, meet financial and tax obligations, resolve disputes, maintain safety and audit records, and comply with applicable law. Some charging, receipt, and billing records may need to be retained even after account closure.

8. Your Choices

You can update account details, notification preferences, and communication choices in supported ElectraHub apps. You may request access, correction, deletion, or export of personal information by contacting support.

9. Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, authentication checks, audit logs, and operational monitoring to protect information. No system is perfectly secure, so please report suspected issues promptly.

10. Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@electrahub.com.
