ElectraHub provides a cloud-based EV charging management platform consisting of three primary components:

OCPP Simulator terms

The OCPP Simulator provides a sandboxed environment that mimics real EV charging infrastructure. The following terms apply specifically to simulator usage:

Simulated data only All charging sessions, energy readings, station statuses, and transaction data generated within the simulator are entirely fictional. They do not represent real energy consumption, billing, or grid interactions.
No financial transactions The simulator does not process real payments. Any pricing, billing, or payment flows shown are for demonstration purposes only. No actual charges will be applied to any payment method during simulation.
Data retention Simulator session data may be automatically purged after 30 days. ElectraHub does not guarantee long-term storage of simulator-generated data.
Transition to production The simulator is designed to replicate the production experience. When you're ready to connect real OCPP-compliant chargers, contact the ElectraHub team for onboarding. Simulator configurations are not automatically migrated.
